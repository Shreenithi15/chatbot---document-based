Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Overview

AI is the simulation of human intelligence in machines that are programmed to think and learn. Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI that provides systems the ability to automatically learn and improve from experience without being explicitly programmed.

Key Domains:
1. Computer Vision: Enables computers to derive meaningful information from digital images, videos, and visual inputs.
2. Natural Language Processing (NLP): Allows computers to understand, interpret, and manipulate human language.
3. Robotics: Blends AI with hardware to execute physical tasks automatically.

Benefits:
- Automates repetitive tasks
- Fast data processing and real-time insights
- Reduces human error in complex decision-making
